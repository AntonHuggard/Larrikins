About

This website is an archive of a comic I drew from 2018 - 2024.

First drawings of Whitaker, Janurary (left) & February (right) 2018

Larrikins started out as a fortnightly comic in Victoria University’s student magazine Salient in 2018.

At the end of the year I built a website to archive the work.

I kept doing it on-and-off for a couple of years afterwards. In 2021 I drew some Bird of the Year posters.

In 2022, Larrikins briefly appeared in Auckland University’s student magazine Craccum.

In 2023, Forest & Bird’s kids magazine Wild Things published Larrikins.

In 2024, Larrikins once again appeared in Salient.
